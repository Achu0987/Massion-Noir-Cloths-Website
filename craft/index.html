Craft and care

Home/Craft

Cloth

Every garment starts with the fabric. We pick heavy weights and long fibres from mills that can tell us where the wool was grown and how it was dyed.

Pattern

One relaxed unisex block, drawn by hand and graded across six sizes. A single pattern means every piece layers with every other.

Cut

We cut in small runs of two hundred or fewer, by hand on the table, so the grain sits where it should and nothing is wasted.

Sew

Stitched in our own studio by people we know. Seams are pressed as they are sewn, which is why the finished garment hangs the way it does.

Finish

Washed, pressed and checked piece by piece before it is folded. If it would not pass our own wardrobe, it does not ship.

Materials

What we make with

Merino wool

Extra-fine, naturally temperature regulating and soft against the skin.

Lambskin leather

Full-grain and vegetable-tanned where possible. Ages with character.

Selvedge denim

Dense, rigid indigo that fades to your own wear pattern.

Washed linen

Pre-washed so it is soft from the first day and never stiff.

Care guide

Make it last

Knitwear
Hand wash cold with wool detergent. Dry flat, away from direct heat. Fold, never hang.
Denim
Wash rarely, cold and inside out. Hang to dry. Spot clean in between.
Leather
Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Condition once a season. Store on a wide hanger.
Tailoring
Dry clean only, and only when needed. Steam to refresh between wears.
