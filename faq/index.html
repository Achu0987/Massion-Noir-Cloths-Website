FAQ

Home/FAQ
How long does delivery take?
Orders ship in 2 working days. Delivery takes 3 to 6 working days across India. You will get tracking by email and SMS.
What is your returns policy?
You can return any unworn piece with tags within 14 days of delivery. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
How do I find my size?
Every product page has a size finder. Enter your height and weight and we suggest a size. Our block is unisex and relaxed, so size down for a closer fit.
Do you ship internationally?
Not yet. We currently ship within India only. Join the newsletter to hear when international shipping opens.
How should I care for my clothes?
Care instructions are on every product page. As a rule: wash cold, wash less and dry flat or on a line.
Can I change or cancel an order?
Yes, within 12 hours of placing it. Write to us with your order number and we will sort it out.
